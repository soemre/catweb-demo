## Start here

A sample guide to choosing tasks, planning breaks, and keeping useful notes. This example shows how a full resource can combine a short overview, practical steps, and links to other information.

## A few useful steps

1. Read the introduction and decide what information you need.
2. Look for the date, destination, or practical detail that answers your question.
3. Take one small step rather than trying to do everything at once.
4. Return to the resource when you want to explore further.

### Before a gathering

Clear information can make arriving easier. In this fictional example, the group explains the space, the programme, and the ways people can take part.

| Question | What a useful guide explains |
| --- | --- |
| Where do I go? | A clearly named venue or online destination |
| What happens first? | A welcome, an introduction, and time to settle in |
| Can I just listen? | The different ways to participate |
