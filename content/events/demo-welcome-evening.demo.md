## What to expect

Meet new people, explore the community, and find an activity that feels right for you. This sample programme combines an informal welcome with time to join an activity at your own pace.

There is no single way to take part. You can come with a friend, arrive on your own, or start by listening. The welcome team introduces the space and helps people find a comfortable place to begin.

## The programme

| Part | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Welcome | Introductions and a short explanation of the space |
| Main activity | A shared activity with room for different interests |
| Break | Time to pause, chat, or use the quiet corner |
| Closing | Share ideas for the next gathering |

### Before you come

- Read the time and location above.
- Bring curiosity rather than any particular experience.
- Choose the level of participation that suits you.

> A good gathering leaves room for different ways of belonging.

You can also read the [demo welcome pack](/resources/demo-welcome-pack-guide/) or explore [ways to contribute](/join/).
